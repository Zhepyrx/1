Lumencraft

Explore and build in an endless voxel world lit by a physical sky, drifting volumetric clouds, soft sun shadows, reflective water and light that spills from every torch.

Graphics quality
Dynamic resolution renders fewer pixels when needed and a temporal upscaler rebuilds full detail, so the frame rate stays at your target.
W A S D move · Space jump, double-tap to fly · Shift sprint · click to break · right-click to place · E blocks · T pause time · R weather · F3 stats

Lumencraft is played with a keyboard and mouse. On this device the world renders, but you can't move around.
