Lumencraft

Explore and build in an endless voxel world lit by a physical sky and drifting volumetric clouds. Wander twenty-four biomes, from autumn maple woods and coral reefs to glowing Lumen Groves and smoking volcanoes, alive with deer, foxes, birds and fish.

Graphics quality
Dynamic resolution renders fewer pixels when needed and a temporal upscaler rebuilds full detail, so the frame rate stays at your target.
W A S D move · Space jump, double-tap to fly · Shift sprint · click to break · right-click to place · E blocks · Tab atlas · P photo mode · F2 screenshot · Ctrl Z undo

Lumencraft is played with a keyboard and mouse. On this device the world renders, but you can't move around.
